SignBridge

An assistive sign-to-speech prototype for deaf and non-speaking users. This app detects 21 hand landmarks with MediaPipe, converts mapped gestures into speech, and lets you collect real labeled samples to train a personal gesture recognizer.

Live Hand Detection Detects one or two hands in real time using MediaPipe in the browser.
Custom Gesture Shortcuts Focuses on useful phrases like Help, Water, Food, Emergency, and Call Family.
Speech Output Converts recognized phrases into audio for real-life communication support.
The included gesture-to-phrase mappings are custom shortcuts, not official ASL translations. Accuracy depends on lighting and sample quality; do not rely on this app for emergencies.
